Hardware Design and Lab.

Includes the fundamental concepts of hardware design and their implementation on the FPGA board(Basys 3).

Course Outline

1. Gate-level modeling

2. Data-flow modeling and Behavioral modeling

3. Sequential circuits & FPGA board

4. Finite State Machines & FPGA board

5. Advanced circuit implementation, Finite State Machine, and FPGA board

6. Integration with different sensors and peripherals

7. Final Project: The students will submit a proposal, then exercise their creativity to build the project.

Note

Introduction to Embedded Systems

The course is divided into theoretical lectures and practical lab sessions. Labs are designed to reinforce lecture content with hands-on experience using microprocessor development boards.

Course Outline

This course will introduce the architecture and design process of embedded systems. We will introduce the operation mechanism of microprocessor software such as registers, I/O ports, addressing modes, timers, and interrupts. Upon mastering microprocessor
principles, the course will proceed to introduce common microprocessor interfaces including UART, I2C, and ADC/DAC. Furthermore, students will be guided to apply embedded operating systems for projects. The objective of this course is to cultivate students' capabilities in embedded system design, enabling them to independently develop systems tailored to specific requirements.

課程共12個實驗，完成每個實驗5%: 60%
Final Test: 40%
